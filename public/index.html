Security and trust

Built so you and your client can trust the link.

  • Secure sign-in

    Email link, Google or GitHub, plus optional two-step sign-in with an authenticator app.

  • Private vault

    Briefs, contracts and files stay in each project. Share a file with a link that ends after 7 days.

  • Sealed links

    Each document is sealed when you send it. The receipt shows its fingerprint, so any change is visible.

  • We never hold your money

    Clients pay you directly by PromptPay, bank, bKash or your own Stripe link.

  • Clauses by country

    Clause templates for Thailand, Bangladesh, Malaysia and Singapore. Templates, not legal advice.

  • Your client's language

    Your client can read estimates and documents in English, Bengali or Thai.

SignedScope is not a law firm or a legal e-signature service. Row Level Security keeps every account's data separate.

Questions

Straight answers.

Does the AI set my price?

No. It estimates the hours. Your price is hours × your rate + your buffer, and you can change any line.

Is the signature legally binding?

It records who approved what and when, with a fingerprint of the exact document. It is not a certified e-signature, so check your local rules for large contracts.

Do you handle the payments?

No. Your client pays you directly by PromptPay, bank, bKash or your own Stripe link. SignedScope never holds or moves money.

Which languages?

English, Bengali and Thai for estimates and client documents. More of the app is being translated.

Is my data private?

Each account can only see its own data. Files sit in a private vault, and share links expire.
