Questions

Is this a legally binding e-signature?

No. It is a signed record of what the client approved, rejected and when. Use it next to your contract, not instead of a regulated e-signature service. The signer's identity is what they type in.

Does it work for Upwork or Fiverr projects?

Yes, for the record and the diff. Leave the Pay link empty and add the approved amount as a new milestone on the platform, because platform work has to be paid there.

Where is my data?

On the free tier, inside the link. The server signs it and keeps no copy, so save your receipt links. Pasted messages are sent to the server to draft lines and are not stored.

Does SignedScope take a cut of payments?

No. Free links open your own payment page. Pro will add invoices through SignedScope's Stripe; any fee will be shown before it opens.

Wasn't this called ScopeLock?

Yes. Another company already sells a product called ScopeLock, so this one is now SignedScope. Links you already sent still work.
